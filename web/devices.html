Good to know

  • Click a device's name to rename it, e.g. "Mum's phone" or "Living room TV".
  • Most phones, and newer laptops, use a private address that hides who made them, and some change it now and then. A device you already know can show up as new after one of those changes.
  • Sleeping devices don't always answer, so a phone in someone's pocket may show as offline even though it's still connected.
  • With the Mac app running, you get a notification when a device joins that hasn't been seen before.
