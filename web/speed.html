Download by time of day

Evenings are usually the slowest, since that's when everyone in the area is online. A big dip there points to an overloaded ISP rather than your Wi-Fi.

About Wi-Fi limits

A speed test over Wi-Fi can't go faster than the Wi-Fi link itself. When a result gets close to what your Wi-Fi link can carry, it's marked limited by Wi-Fi and left out of the averages, because it says more about your signal than your ISP.

For results you'd send to your ISP, run a few checks near the router or on Ethernet.
