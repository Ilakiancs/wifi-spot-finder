Channel scanner

Scans the Wi-Fi networks around you and checks how crowded your router's channel is. Neighbours on the same channel take turns with you, which slows everyone down. Moving to a quieter channel is free and often helps.

What's on the air

Each hump is a network: its width is the slice of spectrum it uses, and its height is how strong it is here. Yours is drawn in blue.
