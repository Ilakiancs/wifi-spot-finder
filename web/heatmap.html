Tips

  • A photo of a hand-drawn sketch works fine. So does a screenshot of a listing's floor plan, or the blank grid.
  • Record in every room, plus a few spots along the way between the router and the weakest room. More spots give a better map.
  • The colours fade where you haven't measured. The map only guesses near real readings.
  • A mesh node needs a good link back to the router. That's why the suggestion sits where signal is still good (about −67 dBm or better) but as close to your dead zone as possible.
